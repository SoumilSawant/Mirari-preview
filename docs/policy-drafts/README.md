# Customer policy drafts (NOT approved, NOT published)

These are **starting templates** for the owner and a solicitor to review. They are not legal advice and
they are not loaded into the app. Nothing here may be published, pasted into Admin, or relied on until the
owner has filled every `[PLACEHOLDER]` and a qualified person has approved the final text.

| File | Becomes the Admin policy field |
| --- | --- |
| `terms.md` | `policy.terms` (Booking terms) |
| `refunds.md` | `policy.refunds` (Refunds and changes) |
| `privacy.md` | `policy.privacy` (Privacy notice) |
| `accessibility.md` | `policy.accessibility` |
| `contact.md` | `policy.contact` |

## How they would be used (only after approval)

1. Owner fills the `[PLACEHOLDER]` values with real, confirmed facts. Nothing is invented here.
2. A solicitor reviews each document against current UK law and Mirari's actual operations.
3. The approved text is pasted into Admin, Settings, policies (each field takes up to 15,000 characters).
4. Only then may live start-up use `LIVE_POLICIES_APPROVED=true`.

## Points the reviewer should decide (open questions, not answers)

- **Legal entity.** Is Mirari a company, sole trader or partnership? Registered name, number, address, VAT status.
- **Cancellation rights.** UK consumer rules treat some dated leisure events differently from ordinary online
  purchases. Confirm what applies to Mirari's events and word the refund policy to match.
- **Refunds for cancelled, postponed or changed events.** The older supplied draft saying "no refunds" is not
  suitable for these cases. Decide who gets a refund, when, and whether fees are returned.
- **Fees and tax.** The app enters prices inclusive of mandatory fees and applicable taxes. Confirm tax treatment.
- **Data protection.** Confirm controller details, lawful bases, retention periods, processors (Stripe, Resend,
  the host) and any international transfers. Decide whether an ICO registration or fee applies.
- **Under-18s, alcohol, photography and filming notices** if relevant to Mirari events.
- **Complaints route** and any alternative dispute resolution body.
