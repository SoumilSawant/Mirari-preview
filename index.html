Four kinds of cultural event

Music, dance, art and festivals, all in London.

  1. Music and live performance

    Classical mornings and fusion concerts, with music that grandparents and teenagers can sit through together.

  2. Dance and movement

    Lavani, folk dance, Bollywood and contemporary fusion on stage, and workshops where you join in.

  3. Art and creative workshops

    Hands-on sessions in Warli painting, Madhubani art, block printing and other traditional crafts.

  4. Festivals and celebrations

    Diwali and other traditional Indian festivals, held as gatherings that anyone can walk into.

Booking questions

Do I need an account to book?

No. You book as a guest with your name and email, and you can ask for a private link to your tickets at any time.

How will I receive my tickets?

Once payment is confirmed, your QR tickets appear on the confirmation page and a private link is sent to your email.

Are booking fees included?

Yes. The price shown on each ticket includes every mandatory fee, and you see the total before you pay.

What if I lose the email?

Open My tickets and enter your booking email. We send a new private link, which works once and expires after 15 minutes.

What if an event changes?

Updates appear in My tickets, and the refunds and changes page sets out what happens next.

Our philosophy

Tradition inspires us. Creativity connects us. Community defines us. Mirari brings heritage and modern expression together in events that feel authentic, inclusive and memorable.

About Mirari

Mirari is a London-based cultural experiences company. We bring people together through music, dance, art and community celebrations.

Our events run from small creative workshops to full festival days. Each one sets a tradition beside the way people in London practise it now, and each is a room where you can meet others who care about it.
